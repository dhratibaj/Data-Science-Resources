## Tell me about yourself

### ✅ What Managers Are Really Looking For

1. A quick summary of your professional identity
* They want to understand who you are professionally in the first 30–60 seconds. Not your life story, just your role, experience, and specializations.

2. Relevance to the role
* They’re silently evaluating: "Does this person’s background align with the job we’re hiring for?" It’s your chance to draw a direct line between your past work and their current needs.

3. Confidence and communication
* They want to see how clearly and confidently you present yourself. Can you structure your thoughts well? Are you articulate?

4. Highlights, not history
* Think of this as a teaser trailer to your resume—not a scene-by-scene walkthrough. Focus on impactful highlights, achievements, or key strengths.

5. Your “why”
* They're looking for some insight into your motivation. Why are you in this field? Why this role, and why now?

### ❌ Common Misconceptions

1. "I should start with my childhood or college days."
* No need to go that far back unless it’s very relevant to the job. Managers are more interested in your recent and relevant experience.

2. "I should just repeat what’s on my resume."
*They’ve (hopefully) read your resume. This is your chance to tell a story that connects the dots and shows your value in your own words.

3. "It’s just small talk—they don’t really care."
* Not true. This is often the most important question—they're forming their first impression, and it can influence how they perceive your answers to the rest of the interview.

4. "I should cram every project I’ve done into this answer."
* Nope. Pick a few impactful examples and keep it focused. Think quality over quantity.

### ✨ Pro Tip
Treat it like your personal pitch. The ideal answer is around 60-90 seconds, highlights your role, skills, achievements, and ends with a hook—what you’re looking for next and why you're interested in this opportunity.

eg:-
### 🔹 60-Second Elevator Pitch

"I'm a Data Scientist with 2 years of experience, specializing in building GenAI solutions and deploying them using cloud-agnostic architectures. My work spans MLOps, LLMOps, and traditional machine learning, and I've delivered 6–7 projects end-to-end across three client-facing engagements.

One recent project was a GenAI-based price claim validation tool using Azure, which improved document processing speed by 16% and achieved 81% accuracy. I also co-developed a RAG-based knowledge assistant on GCP that cut down manual effort by 60%. In another initiative, I led a predictive modeling project that reduced processing time by 87.5%.

Beyond technical delivery, I often bridge gaps across teams and contribute to deployment workflows using Docker. I adapt quickly, enjoy solving complex problems, and am now looking to join a team that values innovation and keeps pace with AI’s rapid evolution."

### 🔹 90-Second Deep Dive Version

"I'm a GenAI-focused Data Scientist with 2 years of experience delivering scalable, cloud-agnostic AI solutions. My expertise spans MLOps, LLMOps, and both generative and traditional ML approaches. I've worked across three client-facing projects, successfully delivering 6–7 solutions from ideation to production.

Most recently, I developed a GenAI-based MVP for price claim validation using Azure services, which boosted document processing speed by 16% and delivered 81% accuracy. I also played a key role in building a RAG-based knowledge assistant on GCP that reduced manual support effort by 60%. In another standalone project, I designed a predictive model that identified child items based on parent attributes—this reduced processing time by 87.5%, streamlining internal operations.

In addition to model development, I’ve contributed to Docker-based deployments, worked across cross-functional teams, and often act as the bridge between data science, engineering, and product teams. My strength lies in quickly learning new technologies and aligning solutions with business goals. I'm now seeking a role where I can continue building impactful AI products in a fast-paced, forward-thinking environment."
